# Using Viyam

## Set up

1. Choose the exact date bank tracking should start, or use Today / 1 / 3 / 6 / 12 month shortcuts. A future date schedules tracking; earlier alerts are skipped.
2. Choose the day your budget month begins (1–31). Shorter months use their last day.
3. Read the SMS disclosure. Allow optional SMS access or continue with manual entry.
4. If using bank alerts, choose a sender from your inbox, create or select an account, and use **Save & track**. Repeat for the senders you want. Suggestions do not approve tracking automatically.
5. Review the imported records. Notification access is optional.

Sender names are identifiers, not verified bank identities. Carefully check the selected sender and its latest message.

## Record and review

Add manual expenses or income when needed. Imported transactions start unclassified: review each amount and assign a category. Merchant preferences suggest categories for later transactions; they do not silently approve them. A batch review can apply a category to explicitly selected matching pending transactions.

Use **Bank message** on a record to inspect its original SMS. Unsupported messages appear under **Needs review**: choose **Record a transaction**, provide the missing details, or **Ignore message**. Ignored messages remain stored and can be restored through Activity or Settings. Restoring a reversal requires fresh review.

## Budgets and accounts

Set a total monthly budget and optional category limits. Category limits do not automatically sum into the total. Account-specific budgets and the All accounts budget are separate. Use account filters to focus views and reports. You can create custom income and expense categories in Settings or during classification.

Account purposes are labels and do not automatically classify incoming credits as salary. Archive an account to retain its history while stopping its active sender mappings.

## Transfers, investments and reversals

- **Own-account transfers:** confirm the two matching, opposite-direction alerts. A confirmed transfer is excluded from income and spending. With only one SMS, explicitly recording a manual opposite leg is available. Separate bank fees remain expenses.
- **Investments:** confirm contributions or withdrawals through their dedicated workflow. Principal movements stay outside everyday spending and earned income. They are cash movements, not market valuations or inferred profit.
- **Reversals:** select the original purchase and confirm the amount, including partial refunds. A cancellation notice alone does not reduce spending. A linked reversal adjusts the original period. Undo is available.

Check account, amount and direction before confirming links. Candidate matches are suggestions.

## Reports

Reports show recorded income and spending, categories, merchants and investment movements over budget cycles. Transfers and investment principal are separated from ordinary income/spending, and confirmed reversals adjust the original records. Missing imported history can make reports incomplete. These figures are not bank balances or reconciled statements.

## Import more history

Use **Settings > Import history** and choose the exact start date. Existing source IDs are used to avoid duplicate imports while preserving user decisions. Changing sender tracking does not erase existing history. Discovery can show senders outside the chosen date range because sender discovery and transaction import serve different purposes.

## Keep your data

There is no export or restore in this beta. Avoid uninstalling or clearing app storage. See the [installation guide](INSTALLATION.md) before updating an existing development build.
